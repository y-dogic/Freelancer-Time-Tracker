# Reel Opening Styles (6种开场画面)

Knowledge-style talking-head videos (知识类口播) lose most viewers in the first 5 seconds because the opening is a static person talking. The fix is not a new script for the whole video. It's making the *first 3–5 seconds* visually move while the words land one beat at a time.

This skill has two parts:
1. **The hook script**, which is the same 3 beats in every style.
2. **The 6 opening edit styles**, which are different ways to animate those 3 beats.

---

## Part 1: The 3-beat hook script

Every opening in the source video uses the same three lines, each shown as on-screen text while it is spoken:

| Beat | Job | Example (source) | Example (English) | Text treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. **Who/what** (~1s) | Name the audience or topic so the right people stop | 知识类口播 | "Talking-head creators" | Small, white |
| 2. **Pain** (~1–1.5s) | State the problem they already feel | 5秒完播率低？ | "Losing viewers in 5 seconds?" | Medium, **red/accent**, often with a "?" |
| 3. **Promise + number** (~1.5–2s) | Offer a specific, countable fix | 试试这**6种**画面剪辑效果 | "Try these **6** opening edits" | The number is HUGE and accent-colored, the rest is small |

Rules of thumb:
- Keep each beat to one short line. If it needs two lines, it's two beats.
- The number in beat 3 is the visual climax. Make it the largest element on screen (often 3–5× the other text).
- Use one accent color (the source uses red `#E8364A`-ish) for pain and number only. Everything else is white.
- The visual "event" (mask change, reveal, flash) should land **on the cut between beats**, especially going into beat 3. Motion on the beat change is what resets attention.

When the user gives you their topic, write their 3 beats first, then pick a style.

## Part 2: The 6 opening styles

Each style is a combination of a **background treatment** and a **person/text animation**. All are doable in CapCut / 剪映 with masks (蒙版), auto cut-out (智能抠像), keyframes (关键帧) and text animation presets (文字动画).

### Style 1: Darkened background + person mask animation (背景压暗，人物蒙版动画)
**Look:** Moody background (darkened ~40–60%). The speaker sits inside a **circle mask**. On the pain beat a large grey "?" appears and the circle **shrinks** under it. On the promise beat the circle snaps into a **full cut-out of the person with a white sticker outline (描边)** over the dark background, and a giant red number pops in beside them.
**Best for:** Moody or premium topics, "mystery/question" hooks, a busy background you want to hide.
**Recipe:**
1. Put a background clip/image on the bottom track. Lower brightness/exposure until dark.
2. Overlay the talking-head clip. Add a circle mask (圆形蒙版) around the face/upper body, with slight feather.
3. Beat 2: keyframe the mask (or clip scale) smaller. Add a "?" text in grey, scale-in animation.
4. Beat 3: split the clip. On the second part, remove the mask, apply 智能抠像 cut-out, and add a white stroke (描边) to make a sticker look. Pop in the big number (scale-up/bounce preset).

### Style 2: Person cut-out + background mask animation (人物抠像，背景做蒙版动画)
**Look:** The speaker is cut out and placed on a **different, more on-topic background** (for example a library for a knowledge topic). Text sits above their head. On the promise beat a **torn-paper edge (撕纸) wipes across**, revealing the real room behind them, and a giant red number rises from the bottom.
**Best for:** Plain or messy filming locations, adding "context" to a topic, and a strong scene change on the climax.
**Recipe:**
1. Bottom track: the real footage (uncut). Middle track: themed background image/video. Top track: the same talking-head clip with 智能抠像.
2. Give the middle (themed) background a linear/custom mask. Keyframe it so it wipes away on beat 3. Overlay a torn-paper PNG/sticker along the mask edge so the wipe looks ripped.
3. Beats 1–2: white text top-left (small → medium). Beat 3: huge red number anchored bottom, rise-in animation, with a small white subline across it.

### Style 3: Opening spotlight + person cut-out animation (开场聚光灯特效，人物抠像动画)
**Look:** Starts dark with a **spotlight/vignette** on the speaker. Key words (e.g. "5秒") are placed **behind the person's head** (text sandwiched between background and cut-out, for depth), with the rest of the line in front. The light opens up to full brightness on the promise beat, with the number big and red beside the person.
**Best for:** Making a plain frame look three-dimensional, and creators who want a "cinematic" feel.
**Recipe:**
1. Duplicate the clip: bottom = original, top = 智能抠像 cut-out.
2. Put the big keyword text **between** the two tracks so the head overlaps it. Put the supporting text above the cut-out.
3. Add a spotlight/vignette effect (聚光灯 / 暗角) for the first 1–2 beats and keyframe it off for beat 3.
4. Beat 3: medium shot / slight zoom-in, with the number in red to one side of the face.

### Style 4: Bold type layout + person mask/cut-out (文字大小排版，人物蒙版抠像动画)
**Look:** **Solid black background**, which makes it a typography poster. Text stacks with strong size hierarchy: small white topic, then bigger red pain line, then a giant white number with a small red subline. The speaker appears in a **rounded-rectangle mask** that animates into a **circle** as more text arrives.
**Best for:** Text-heavy hooks, list/number content, clean and minimal brands, and footage with a bad background.
**Recipe:**
1. Black solid background. Talking-head clip on top with a rounded-rectangle mask (矩形蒙版 + 圆角).
2. Beat 2: keyframe the mask to a circle and move it down a bit to make room for text.
3. Stack text top-down with clear size steps (e.g. 1× / 1.5× / 4×). Only the pain line and subline are red.

### Style 5: Opening flash-in + person focus effect (开场闪现动画，人物聚焦特效)
**Look:** Normal full frame. Beat 1 text **flashes in** at chest height. On the pain beat the frame **darkens except a bright circle around the face** (focus/spotlight), and the red text glitches/dissolves out. On the promise beat the full frame snaps back to bright and a big white "6种" flashes in.
**Best for:** Footage that is already good-looking, when you want minimal editing work, and energetic delivery.
**Recipe:**
1. Text with a "flash"/"闪现" in-animation (quick opacity strobe or scale pop). Place text over the lower chest, not the face.
2. Beat 2: add a radial mask/vignette (or 聚焦 / 局部聚光 effect) centered on the face, darkening the outside. Use a glitch/dissolve out-animation on the red text.
3. Beat 3: remove the effect on the cut (the brightness jump *is* the attention reset), and flash in the big number.

### Style 6: TV texture + text animation presets (画面电视纹理，文字动画预设)
**Look:** A **retro TV / scanline texture** over the footage. Text is **vertical (竖排)** and types/stamps in character by character: topic on the left in white, pain line on the right in red. On the promise beat the background **blurs out** and a giant red "6 / 种" fills the center with a small white subline.
**Best for:** Chinese/vertical typography, nostalgic or editorial aesthetics, and standing out from horizontal-subtitle reels.
**Recipe:**
1. Add a TV/VHS/scanline effect (电视纹理 / 老电视) at low-moderate intensity.
2. Vertical text boxes and a per-character in-animation preset (打字机 / 逐字 / 弹入).
3. Beat 3: blur effect (模糊) on the footage and the number centered and huge, above the blur.

## Choosing a style

Ask (or infer) two things: **How good is the footage/background?** and **What's the brand vibe?**

| Situation | Recommend |
|---|---|
| Messy or plain background | 2 (swap background) or 4 (black poster) |
| Good-looking footage, want low effort | 5 |
| Moody, premium, mysterious topic | 1 or 3 |
| List / number / text-heavy hook | 4 |
| Want depth and a cinematic feel | 3 |
| Editorial, retro, or Chinese vertical typography | 6 |
| Posting a series | Rotate styles across posts so the feed doesn't look repetitive, but keep the 3-beat script and accent color consistent for brand recognition |

Offer a primary pick plus one alternative, with one sentence each on why.

## What to give the user

When asked to plan or improve a reel opening, respond with:

1. **Hook script**: their 3 beats, with the on-screen text for each (mark the accent words).
2. **Chosen style** and why (plus one alternative).
3. **Shot-by-shot timeline** for the first ~4–5 seconds, like:
   ```
   0.0–1.0s  Beat 1 "…"   | background: … | person: … | text: small white, top-left, fade-in
   1.0–2.3s  Beat 2 "…?"  | … | text: red, medium, + grey "?"
   2.3–4.5s  Beat 3 "…N…" | <the transition event on the cut> | number: huge red, bounce-in
   ```
4. **Editor steps** adapted from the recipe above for their tool (CapCut/剪映 by default. Translate to Premiere/Final Cut/After Effects terms if they use those: mask → track matte / shape mask, 智能抠像 → Remove Background / Rotobrush, text animation preset → text animator / MOGRT).

If the user wants the edit actually rendered rather than a plan, and a video-editing skill (such as a Remotion-based reel maker) is available, hand off the hook script and the chosen style's layer stack (background → masked/cut-out person → text layers, plus the beat-3 transition) to that skill.

Keep advice concrete. "Make it engaging" is useless, but "circle mask shrinks under a grey '?' at 1.0s" is something the person can edit.
