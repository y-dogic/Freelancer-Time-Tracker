# 爆款拆解框架 / Breakdown framework

Seven dimensions, in report order. Each has the questions to answer and what evidence to cite.
The guiding questions are prompts for thinking, not a checklist to recite. Answer the ones the
video actually gives you material for, and skip the rest rather than padding.

## 1. 选题逻辑 — topic logic

- **核心命题**: one sentence: what is the video *really* arguing? It's often different from the title
  (title "提升幸运的6个方法" → real thesis "你无法决定所有条件，但可以调整自己与机会相遇的方式").
- **目标人群**: describe a *state*, not a demographic ("努力了却一直不顺的人" beats "18-30岁女性").
  List 3-4 concrete situations the viewer is in.
- **为什么值得做**: is it a perennial need (钱/健康/关系/成长/颜值), a current trend, a
  counter-intuitive take on a common belief, or a big vague word (幸运/松弛感/情绪价值) turned
  into something actionable?
- **提供什么价值**: 实用 (steps you can apply), 认知 (a new frame), 情绪 (being seen / comforted / pumped), or
  娱乐/社交货币. Usually one primary plus one secondary.

## 2. 脚本与画面 — script and visuals

- **脚本结构**: rebuild the outline as a tree with approximate timestamps (开篇 / 前置框架 / 主体 n 点 / 结尾).
  Name the structure type: 总分总, 问题-原因-方案, 清单体, 故事-转折-道理, 对比/反差, 实验/挑战, 测评.
- Note any **前置论证** (a premise that makes the main points believable) and where the **节奏变化**
  happens (a story, a joke, or a pattern break inserted mid-way to reset attention).
- **章节表**: for each section give the time range, the order of content inside it
  (引用→定义→类比→结论…), and what the example or visual is doing (making the abstract concrete,
  adding proof, adding emotion).
- **画面与剪辑**: shot types (口播近景/中景, B-roll, 素材混剪, 屏幕录制, 字幕卡/大字报),
  camera movement (固定/推拉/手持跟拍), cut rhythm (use shots.json: average shot length,
  cuts per 10s, whether the rhythm speeds up at key points), on-screen text (花字, keyword
  highlights, progress or chapter labels), and the visual identity (setting, wardrobe, color).
  Say how the visuals *serve* the script, for example "每个方法出现时都有大字卡 → 帮观众建立进度感".
- Be honest about the limits. Frames are sampled, so subtle camera moves and transitions may be
  missed. Say "约" for timings, and "未逐帧核验" when you infer camera movement.

## 3. 完整的开头 — the opening (usually 0-15s, the hook usually 0-3s)

- **前 3 秒的钩子** (quote the exact words plus what's on screen). Common types:
  结果前置 / 数字承诺 ("6个方法") / 反常识 / 痛点提问 / 身份点名 ("如果你是…") /
  冲突或悬念 / 视觉奇观或反差 / 权威背书 (引用书、名人、数据).
- **转折进入主体**: the exact line or moment where the opening hands over to the body, and what bridges the two.
- **留人机制**: how it keeps you watching: an open loop (a promise paid off later), a progress
  signal (第一个…), a "最后一个最重要", or a teased payoff.
- Reproduce the opening as a short beat sheet:
  `0:00 [画面] … / [台词] …` → `0:03 …` → `0:0x 进入主体`.

## 4. 金句 — quotable lines

- Pull the 2-5 lines most likely to be screenshotted, commented, or reused. Quote them verbatim
  from the transcript or the on-screen text, with timestamps.
- For each one, say why it works: 对仗/反转/具体化/把抽象词翻译成日常场景/替观众说出心里话.
- Write **one sentence that captures the whole video**. It can be the video's own line, or your
  distillation, labeled as such.

## 5. 情绪与人群 — emotion and audience

- Which **group** is moved, and which line or moment moves them.
- Which **emotions** fire, and in what sequence: 焦虑/不甘 → 被理解 → 希望/掌控感 → 行动冲动 is a common arc.
  Others: 好奇, 爽感, 愤怒/不平, 怀旧, 治愈, 羡慕, 优越感, 认同.
- **Does it offer a way out?** Concrete actions, a mindset shift, or only catharsis? Videos that
  pair emotional resonance with something actionable tend to get saved and shared.

## 6. 传播逻辑 — distribution logic

Use the data in summary.md (or what the user provided). Never invent numbers. If there's no data,
say so, give hypotheses, and say which numbers would confirm them.

Interpreting ratios (rough heuristics for Douyin and Xiaohongshu. Benchmarks vary by niche, so
treat them as directional):
- **收藏/点赞 high** (≳20-30%): 实用/干货/清单 → people save it to use later. This drives long-tail search traffic.
- **评论/点赞 high** (≳5-10%): 争议, 共鸣 to share, a question that invites answers, or a giveaway prompt.
- **转发/点赞 high** (≳10%): 社交货币: "转给需要的人", 替我说话, or a family/couple/friend topic.
- **点赞 ≫ 粉丝数**: the video broke out of its follower base into recommendations. Explain what in
  the content let it travel (a broad topic, low comprehension cost, a strong hook).
- Also consider: length vs completion (a long video that went viral means strong 留存设计),
  title and hashtags (#独立思考 #心理学 → which traffic pools they target), 发布时间, BGM/热点 tie-ins,
  and the comment-bait in the ending.

Structure this section as: 数据表现 → 读出的信号 → 驱动传播的内容机制 → 不确定之处.

## 7. 可复用的框架逻辑 — reusable framework

Turn the mechanisms above into templates the user can fill in for their own videos:

| 模块 | 模板 | 本视频示例 |
|---|---|---|
| 选题 | 把「大而玄的词」拆成「可执行的 N 个方法」 | 幸运 → 6 个方法 |
| 开头 | 数字承诺 + 反常识论断 + 权威引用 | … |
| 主题/结构 | 前置框架（先让观众接受前提）+ N 点清单 | … |
| 金句 | 「你无法 X，但可以 Y」 | … |
| 钩子/留人 | … | … |
| 结尾 | 回扣全部要点 + 拔高到认知层 + 互动引导 | … |

Then:
- **最值得借鉴的 1-3 点**, ranked, each with one sentence on *why it transfers*.
- **你可以怎么用**: 2-3 concrete adaptations. If the user told you their niche or account, apply it
  to that; otherwise give examples from a few common niches.
- **不建议照搬的地方** (optional): elements that depend on this creator's persona, resources, or timing.
